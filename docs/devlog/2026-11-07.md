**Found:** the deployment controller garbage-collected every pod whose owner wasn't a Deployment. Stage 2's Job controller would have had its pods deleted within moments. No test saw it because the X-6 test's controller didn't own pods. A test now creates a pod owned by a test-only kind against the real `chronosd` app, and it failed (the pod was gone in under 2 s). **Fix:** the controller handles only pods with its `chronos.dev/deployment` label (every pod it has ever made has one), and its owner index has its own name. This is the one change to `src/` in this block. It's a bug fix, not an accommodation for the test extensions: the extensions still need no production changes.
